● Based on my web search, Katoomba Police was a local station of the NSW Police Force, not a separate municipal police department.

  Key Historical Findings:

  Centralized State Police Since 1862

  - 1862: The Police Regulation Act amalgamated all existing police forces (Mounted Police, Foot Police, Water Police) into one unified NSW Police 
  Force under the Inspector-General
  - No municipal police: The only experiment with municipal police in NSW was Sydney (1842-1846), which failed
  - By the 1890s: All policing in NSW was conducted under the centralized state police force

  Katoomba Police Station
