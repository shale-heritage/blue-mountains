● What was "Police Court" in NSW ca. 1900?

  "Police Court" was informal/colloquial terminology for what were formally called "Courts of Petty Sessions" in NSW. These were:

  - Local magistrate's courts handling minor criminal offences (theft, drunkenness, disorderly conduct, assault, licensing violations)
  - Presided over by Police Magistrates (paid, trained officials) or Justices of the Peace
  - The lowest tier of the judicial system - more serious crimes went to District or Supreme Court
  - Very common in newspaper reports of the era

  The term "Police Court" likely reflects that these courts were increasingly presided over by Police Magistrates (professional, paid magistrates)
  rather than unpaid JPs by the 1890s-1900s.

  For the taxonomy: Should I add "Police Court" as a synonym for "Courts of Petty Sessions" or similar existing tag, or add it as a new entity?
