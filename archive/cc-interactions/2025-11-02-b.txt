  Historical Definition of Reserves

  Public reserves were parcels of Crown land set aside by the NSW Government for specific public purposes, including:
  - Recreation grounds - for general public recreation, often later used for showgrounds
  - Rifle reserves - land designated specifically for rifle practice and military training
  - Aboriginal reserves - land set aside for Aboriginal use (separate category)
  - National parks - natural landscape preservation (from 1879 onwards)
