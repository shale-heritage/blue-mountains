# Proposed Community Institutions Hierarchy

## Overview

Based on contextual analysis, the following tags should be reclassified under **"Community institutions"** as the parent category. These are civic/social organisations and venues (not commercial, not government, not religious).

---

## Proposed Structure

### Community institutions (NEW PARENT)

#### 1. Halls & Meeting Places
*Physical venues used by community for meetings, entertainment, social events*

- **School of Arts** (currently under "School" - MOVE)
- **Katoomba School of Arts** (currently under "School of Arts" - MOVE)
- Odd Fellows' Hall
- I.O.O.F. Hall *(Note: Same as Odd Fellows - IOOF = Independent Order of Odd Fellows)*
- Masonic Hall
- Clarke's Hall
- Waudby's Hall
- Mount Victoria Hall

#### 2. Progress Associations/Committees
*Civic improvement and advocacy organisations*

- Progress committees (generic parent?)
- Katoomba Progress Association
- Leura Progress Association
- Wentworth Falls Progress Association
- Mount Victoria Progress Committee
- Megalong Progress Committee

#### 3. Friendly Societies/Lodges
*Mutual aid and fraternal organisations*

- **Lodges** (already exists as parent - keep as-is)
  - Druid's Lodge (already under Lodges ✓)
  - Mountaineer Lodge

#### 4. Cultural & Recreational Societies
*Non-sports social organisations*

- Horticulture society
- Katoomba Amateur Dramatic Club
- Chess and Draughts Club
