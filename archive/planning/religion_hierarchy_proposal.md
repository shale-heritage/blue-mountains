# Proposed Religion Hierarchy

## Current Structure (BEFORE)

```
Church (parent)
├── Wesleyan Church
├── St Hilda's Church
├── Congregational Church
├── Roman Catholic Church
├── Methodist Church
└── Katoomba Congregational Church

School (parent) - INCORRECT PLACEMENT
└── Sunday school
```

---

## Proposed Structure (AFTER)

### Religion (NEW MASTER PARENT)

#### 1. Church (subcategory)
*Christian church buildings and denominations*

- Wesleyan Church
- St Hilda's Church
- Congregational Church
- Roman Catholic Church
- Methodist Church
- Katoomba Congregational Church

#### 2. Sunday school
*Religious education for children - sibling to Church, not child*

Direct child of Religion (not under Church) because:
- Not a church building
- Not a denomination
- Religious education program (could be interdenominational)
- Related to churches, but distinct category

---

## Hierarchy Diagram

```
Religion (new master parent)
├── Church (existing parent, now child of Religion)
│   ├── Wesleyan Church
│   ├── St Hilda's Church
│   ├── Congregational Church
│   ├── Katoomba Congregational Church
│   ├── Roman Catholic Church
│   └── Methodist Church
└── Sunday school (moved from "School" parent)
```

---

## Changes Required

1. **Create new parent:** Religion
2. **Modify existing:** Church (change from top-level parent to child of Religion)
3. **Move tag:** Sunday school (from parent=School to parent=Religion)

---

## Future Extensibility

This structure allows for future additions:
- Other denominations/churches
- Religious education programs
- Religious societies/organisations
- Clergy/ministers (if needed)

---

## Alternative Considered

**Alternative:** Make Sunday school a child of Church
- **Rejected because:** Sunday schools were often interdenominational or community-based, not always tied to a specific church building
