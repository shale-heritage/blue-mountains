Example: 'Hotels' appears in:
  1. Agents > Organizations > Hospitality businesses > Hotels
  2. Built Environment > Accommodation buildings > Hotels
  3. Alcohol & Temperance > Alcohol-related venues > Hotels
  4. Tourism & Accommodation > Accommodation > Hotels

This enables:
  - Flexible querying from multiple perspectives
  - Getty AAT compatibility (primary facets)
  - Thematic exhibitions and tours (thematic groupings)
