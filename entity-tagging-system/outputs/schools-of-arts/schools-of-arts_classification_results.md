## Methodology Notes

**Linguistic Heuristic Framework:**

**Spatial indicators → Building classification**
- Locative prepositions (at, in, near)
- Events occurring at location
- Physical descriptions (reading room, library, billiard table)
- Construction/renovation references
- Physical browsing/visiting activities

**Agency indicators → Organisation classification**
- Ownership and management
- Financial operations (debt, revenue, subscriptions)
- Service provision (library services, periodicals)
- Membership and subscriptions
- Committee meetings and governance
- Officer elections and roles
- Staffing and employment
- Event organisation and programming

**Both → When single mention contains both spatial and agency indicators**
- Example: "Committee meeting held at the reading room"
  - Spatial: physical location (reading room)
  - Agency: organisational governance (committee meeting)
