## Notes

- The distinction between 'Hotel licensing' and 'Publican's licensing' is important for research purposes
- 'Hotel licensing' refers to licenses for hotel buildings/establishments
- 'Publican's licensing' refers to licenses for individuals (publicans) to serve alcohol
- Some articles discuss both types, hence both tags are appropriate
- The hierarchies are already implemented in the taxonomy (Activities > Regulatory processes > Licensing)
