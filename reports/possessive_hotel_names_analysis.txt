OVERALL GUIDANCE
================================================================================

Official trading names:
  - Consistently capitalised in sources
  - Used as proper nouns
  - May appear in advertisements, legal notices

Informal references:
  - Sources mention proprietor by name and role
  - References like 'at Brown's hotel' (lowercase)
  - Text discusses landlord/licensee/proprietress by name

If informal, retag as:
  - Hotel (generic) for the establishment
  - Person's name under Hotelliers for the proprietor
