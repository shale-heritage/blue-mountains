**Date:** 2025-10-31

**Purpose:** Analyse all items tagged with "Family Hotel" variants to establish proper hierarchy and naming conventions for this hotel subtype.

**Background:** "Family Hotel" was a historical category of hotels that marketed themselves as family-friendly establishments (as distinct from hotels primarily serving as drinking establishments). The current taxonomy shows inconsistencies in how these are organized.

### Naming Conventions Observed

Historical practice shows hotel names changed with ownership:
- **Owner-specific names:** "Fryer's Family Hotel" (when Fryer family owned it)
- **Location-based names:** "Katoomba Family Hotel" (permanent/official name)
- **Colloquial names:** "the Family Hotel" (when context makes location clear)

This pattern is consistent with other hotels in the collection (e.g., "Mrs Long's Hotel" vs formal hotel names).

### 5. Notes for Consolidation Decisions

**Historical context:** "Family Hotel" was a marketing term distinguishing family-friendly establishments from hotels primarily serving alcohol. These hotels emphasized accommodation, meals, and respectability suitable for women and children.

**Naming practice:** Hotels often took the owner's name informally (Fryer's, Mrs Long's) but had official registered names (Katoomba Family Hotel).

**Getty AAT alignment:** Family hotels represent a functional subtype of hotels, similar to how boarding houses are distinguished from hotels. The hierarchical organization recognizes this semantic distinction.
